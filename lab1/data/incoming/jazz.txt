Jazz is a genre of music that originated in the African-American communities of New Orleans, Louisiana, in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, with its roots in blues and ragtime. Since the 1920s Jazz Age, it has been recognized as a major form of musical expression. It is characterized by swing and blue notes, complex chords, call-and-response vocals, polyrhythms and improvisation. Jazz has roots in West African cultural and musical expression and in African-American music traditions.
